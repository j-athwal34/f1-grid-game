F1 Grid Game

    About

    Guess the finishing order of F1 Drivers from real races. See how many levels you can reach, with each level getting increasingly difficult.

    Featured Race

    Play the featured race mode, where players can challenge a single race venue across random seasons. Track refreshes daily, come back tomorrow for a new challenge!

    How to Play

    Each level shows drivers from a random Grand Prix. Tap them into finishing order, tap again to undo your last pick. Submit a correct guess to advance to the next level. Get one wrong and you'll have to restart. Reach level 10 to win!

    First load can take up to a minute after pressing start. Stay patient!
